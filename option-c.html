§ 02 / Work P. 03

Roles, in long form.

A selection of recent work — 2020 → 2026

Article 01 — Present

Vendidit Inc.

Senior Software Engineer

May 2024 — Present · Remote

Designed a robust multi-vendor commerce system from the ground up — seller onboarding, inventory and customer management woven into a single coherent platform. Wired SendGrid into the spine for real-time buyer-seller email notifications, instrumented the runtime with AWS CloudWatch, and pushed unit-test coverage to 53% with Jest.

Most of the engineering joy lived in the auction layer: real-time bidding paired with live video streaming, engineered to stay fast and fair as the platform scaled to support thousands of concurrent users. The role is as much cross-functional craft as it is code — aligning engineering decisions with the commercial reality the business is chasing.

Node.js NestJS PostgreSQL AWS Lambda React Next.js TypeScript
Article 02

Cefalo Bangladesh

Senior Software Engineer

Dec 2021 — Apr 2024 · Hybrid

Two and a half years inside Norwegian product engineering — building microservice-based web apps for the rental marketplace leid.no with Node.js and Next.js, including a QR-code-driven borrowing flow that turned the physical handoff of tools into a frictionless tap. Architected and maintained a subscription system in PHP / Laravel / Node / Express used across multiple sites including friflyt.no, serving a footprint of 5M subscribers.

On the data side I served Elasticsearch through AWS Lambda and shipped a handful of custom Node modules that stuck. The engineering culture rewarded rigour, which is how the team and I held 82.4% unit-test coverage with Jest across services that genuinely needed to stay up.

Node.js Express PHP Laravel AWS TypeScript Dato CMS Elasticsearch Lambda
